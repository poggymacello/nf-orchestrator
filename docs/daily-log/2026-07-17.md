# 2026-07-17 — Day 5

**Focus:** Observe the real state sequence a deployment goes through and reconcile it with the
lifecycle diagram.

**Studied:** ETSI NFV SOL003 VNF Lifecycle Management (`instantiationState`,
`LcmOperationStateType`) and how O-RAN's O2 interface aligns to it.
Source: https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_gs/NFV-SOL/001_099/003/03.05.01_60/gs_NFV-SOL003v030501p.pdf,
https://www.o-ran.org/specifications

**Did myself:** Redeployed the scratch chart from Day 4 onto the existing cluster. Success path:
`kubectl get pods -w` showed `Pending` → `ContainerCreating` → `Running` (1/1), `helm status`
reported `STATUS: deployed`. Failure path: upgraded to a nonexistent image tag and watched
`Pending` → `ContainerCreating` → `ErrImagePull` → `ImagePullBackOff`, persisting.

**Got stuck on:** Expected `helm status` to reflect the failure. It stayed `STATUS: deployed`
through the entire failed rollout — Helm only confirms the manifest was applied, not that the pod
is healthy. Pod phase is the only signal that actually tells you the deploy failed, matching what
I ran into on Day 4 but now confirmed at the release-status level specifically.

**Learned:** The project's four lifecycle states aren't invented — `NOT_INSTANTIATED` and
`INSTANTIATED` come directly from ETSI NFV SOL003's `instantiationState`, and `INSTANTIATING`/
`FAILED` collapse SOL003's `STARTING`/`PROCESSING`/`FAILED` operation states into one enum each.
O-RAN's O2 interface manages NF deployments by aligning to these same ETSI NFV SOL APIs.
